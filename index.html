परिचय

A shrine born from a Samadhi

Mukti Dham Mukam is the holiest pilgrimage site of the Bishnoi community — a white-marble temple near Mukam village in Bikaner district, Rajasthan, built over the samadhi of Guru Jambheshwar Bhagwan, who founded the Bishnoi Panth and its 29 principles in 1485 at nearby Samrathal Dhora.

Set among sand dunes and khejri groves, the temple grounds are home to deer, blackbuck and peacocks that roam freely — a living reminder of the community's founding vow to protect all living beings.
